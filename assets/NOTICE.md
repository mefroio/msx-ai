# Mascot provenance

`msx-ai-robot.png` is original project artwork generated for MSX-AI on
2026-08-07 with OpenAI image generation. A user-provided Alamy image
(image ID `KN34YK`) was shown during the design discussion as broad visual
inspiration for a friendly retro robot; it was not supplied to the image
generator and no source pixels were copied or edited.

The final prompt brief requested a square, original 1980s-inspired robot
mascot with a cream plastic shell, blue screen face, red and cyan accents,
transparent/plain light background, and a clearly readable `MSX` chest mark.
The resulting PNG was resized to 512 x 512 without changing its composition.
