# Authorship and project origin

MSX-AI was conceived and created by **Rodrigo Galhardi M. Garcia**.

Rodrigo originated the project's central integration concept: expose a common
Model Context Protocol interface not only to an emulator, but also to a real
MSX computer through an agent executing on the MSX itself, with the host and
target connected through a transport-independent TCP/IPv4 path.

The architecture, product direction, hardware-control goals, and decision to
keep openMSX, physical MSX hardware, TCP, and individual adapter brands as
independent optional layers were established under his direction.

Development has used AI-assisted engineering, testing, and documentation. That
assistance does not alter the attribution of the project concept and direction
recorded above.
